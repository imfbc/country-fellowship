Mother's Day

Pastor Charles · May 10, 2026

“God invented Guilt so Mothers could be everywhere at once.” Children were asked what is love? A little girl answered, “Love is when your mommy reads you a bedtime story. True love is when she does not skip and pages.” Parenting does not come with an instruction manual…That’s why He gave us a mother.” Maybe your mother did not reflect the qualities God. God has used women teachers or neighbors…. Mom’s and Women have had a positive influence on so many of us… I want to talk to you about the importance of being a positive believer and making a difference each day. 70% of what we hear about our culture is negative. People need to hear some positive truths. How?

  • Principles #1. Make Up Your Mind to be Happy… Phil. 4:12
  • Principle #2 Accept the Irritations of Life. Song of Solomon 2:15 
  • Principle #3 Take Care of Your Body
  • Principle #4 Exercise Your Mind…Romans 12:2
  • Principle #5 Give to Give Again... Acts 20:35
  • Principle #6. Smile; Be Positive & Pleasant “Kind words are like honey- enjoyable and healthful.” Proverbs 16:24
  • Principle #7. Work on Today Matthew 6:33-34
  • Principle #8 Set Some Goals Phil. 3:13
  • Principle #9. Have a Daily Quiet Time Psalm 46:10
  • Principle #10 Do Not Be Afraid 2 Timothy 1:7

Happy Mother’s Day!  Blessings to You – Pastor Charles
