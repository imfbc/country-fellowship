Why We Give

God is generous — and so He calls us to be as well. Generosity is not about obligation; it's an expression of gratitude and trust. When we give, we participate in what God is doing in our community and beyond.

Your giving supports the ministry of Country Fellowship Church: our Sunday gatherings, community outreach, missions, and the ongoing work of making disciples.
