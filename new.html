Welcome to Country Fellowship

You Belong Here

A Place to Meet Jesus & Find Community

Country Fellowship Church is a place where people can meet Jesus, engage in life-giving community, and everyone is welcome. Whether you're new to faith, returning after some time away, or just curious — we're glad you're here.

"We are a community rooted in the Word, committed to one another, and passionate about finishing well for Jesus."

Our Sunday services last about 60 minutes and include a blend of contemporary and traditional music, a Scripture-centered message, and time for prayer. Come as you are — no special attire required.

What to Expect

⏱

About 60 Minutes

Our services are designed to be meaningful and focused — around one hour, including music, Scripture, message, and prayer.

🎵

Contemporary & Traditional Music

We blend modern worship songs with timeless hymns to create a worship experience that feels both fresh and deeply rooted.

📖

Scripture-Centered Message

Every week our pastors teach directly from the Bible, applying ancient truth to everyday life in a way that's clear and relevant.

👋

Warm & Welcoming Community

No dress code. No pressure. Just real people who are glad you came and want to get to know you.

🗓

Sunday Mornings

We gather at 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM every Sunday. Both services offer the same message and worship experience.
